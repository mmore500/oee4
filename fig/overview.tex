\caption{
\textbf{Overview of digital multicell model.}
\footnotesize
Model instantiates a population of digital organisms on toroidal grid.
Cells may form multicell groups --- shown as color-coded patches ($a$).
Cells replicate by copying program content into a chosen neighbor cell, and may choose to grow their existing multicell group ($c$) --- or found a new group ($d$).
Cell behavior arises from execution of genome instructions which may exchange messages with neighbors, sense local simulation state, and perform cell actions.
Sensory input occurs via a combination of special input registers and signal messages triggered by simulation conditions (panel $f$).
In addition to external stimuli (i.e., message passing and simulation events), program code can also trigger arbitrary self-stimuli.
Instantiating multicells within a unified spatial realm with dense biotic interaction introduces potential for biotic selection because multicells constitute each other's environmental context (panel $f$).
}
