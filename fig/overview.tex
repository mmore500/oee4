\caption{
\textbf{Digital multicell model.}
\footnotesize
Digital organisms populate cells on a toroidal grid ($a$), competing to occupy limited space.
Cells may grow multicell groups (color-coded patches; $b$).
Replication occurs by asexual budding, replacing a chosen neighbor ($c$) --- either growing the parent's existing group ($d$) or founding a new group ($e$).
Heritable genetic programs analogous to biological genomes control cell behaviors by activating submodules in response to local signals (e.g., neighbor actions, incoming messages, etc.; $f$).
Because multicells constitute each other's environmental context, potential arises for biotic selection feedbacks ($g$).
}
