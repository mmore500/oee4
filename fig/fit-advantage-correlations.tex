\caption{
\textbf{Niche construction co-occurs with complexity across replicates.}
\footnotesize
To test generality of association between self adaptation and complexity observed in the case study strain, we tested for correlation across replicates.
Self adaptation is calculated as the difference between fitness in self context versus co-evolved context.
Across early stints ($\leq50$), self adaptation co-occurred with both genotype complexity (blue, \ref{fig:fit-advantage-correlations:genotype}) and phenotype complexity (blue, \ref{fig:fit-advantage-correlations:phenotype}).
While association with genotype complexity dissipated in later stints, association with phenotype complexity strengthened.
No correlations arose between complexity and fitness (yellow, \labelcref{fig:fit-advantage-correlations:genotype,fig:fit-advantage-correlations:phenotype}.
Pearson correlation and two-tail Wald tests reported.
Supplementary Figure \ref{fig:fit-advantage-correlations-supp}.
