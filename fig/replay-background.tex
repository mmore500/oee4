\caption{%
\textbf{Constructed niche selects to maintain genotype complexity.}
\footnotesize
We performed short one-stint replay trials on case study strain from stints 33 through 100 ($n=67$), in either co-evolved or self context.
Endpoint genotype complexity was measured via single-site knockout fitness assays.
To enhance standing variation and weaken inherent self-context effects, replayed case study genomes were subject to moderate mutagenization --- dosed to maintain some genomes functionally intact (\ref{fig:replay-background:design}).
Replays in self context maintained significantly higher fenotype complexity than co-evolved context (Mann-Whitney test, \ref{fig:replay-background:moderatedose}).
By contrast, more intense mutagenesis sufficient to degrade all seeded genomes prevented this effect, suggesting specificity to preserve already-evolved complexity (Mann-Whitney test, \ref{fig:replay-background:highdose}\protect\footnotemark).
Supplementary Figure \ref{fig:replay-background-early}) shows replay results for earlier stints.
