\textbf{Adaptation assay design.}
\footnotesize
Fitness measurements were taken on a comparative basis through competition experiments.
Panel \ref{fig:fit-nobbg} overviews basic competition experiment design.
A population is seeded with genomes from two strains in equal proportion,
After a fixed simulation duration, end-state strain abundances are then assessed.
In some cases, to assess context-sensitivity of fitness, competitions were performed under ``biotic background'' conditions where half the initial population is seeded with a background strain (panel \ref{fig:fit-bbg}).
